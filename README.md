A single-page newspaper for Friday 20 November 2026, drawn as a dark night edition: deep-navy paper, cream type, gold statistics, red banner words. Thirty-five verified stories — three leads (Cyclone Akvan slamming the Odisha coast, Kyiv's bridges burning in the night barrage, Asian markets sinking in the AI sell-off), ten illustrated tiles, and twenty-two briefs in full-width columns, with a red DATE CHECK box naming every Thursday item left out. No map: the briefs run full width, and the page is held together by a grid of bordered panels.
